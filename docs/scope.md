# Scope: what qualifies as a trails spot

## Working definition

A facility qualifies for inclusion in the Dig Trails map if it has at least one dedicated line featuring dirt jumps — whether a traditional trails line, a slopestyle feature, or a flow trail with jumps.

The following do **not** qualify on their own:

- Pump tracks
- BMX racing tracks
- Downhill trails (even those with large jumps as obstacles)
- Cross country singletrack
- 4X and dual slalom tracks

A facility with these features alongside qualifying jump lines is still included — but the qualifying jump lines are what makes it eligible.

## Three categories

Every listed spot is assigned to one of three categories. This lets riders quickly distinguish a dedicated community-built trails spot from a commercial bike park or a trail centre with a single jump trail.

### 1. Dedicated trails / jump park

A facility whose primary purpose is dirt jumping. Often community-built and maintained. Examples: S4P Milford, Chicksands jump lines, Burlish Bike Park (jump area), Woburn jump area.

### 2. Bike park with jump lines

A commercial or formally managed bike park that includes dedicated jump lines among broader facilities. Examples: 417 Bike Park, BikePark Wales, Bike Park Kernow.

### 3. Trail centre with jump trail

A primarily XC or downhill trail centre that includes at least one jump trail or flow trail with proper jumps. Examples: Glentress, Cannock Chase, Dalby Forest.

## Official vs informal

For now, only **official / formalised spots** are listed — those with some form of legitimate status (land agreement, lease, council partnership, commercial operation, or established public access).

Informal, illegal, or under-the-radar spots are not listed, for several reasons:

- Listing them publicly could attract attention that threatens their survival
- They cannot be reliably verified or maintained
- The map's purpose is partly to demonstrate the value of formal status

This decision should be revisited periodically and tested with the community.

## Edge cases and ongoing decisions

- **Flow trails specifically** — the line between a progressive jump trail and a flow trail is genuinely contested. The agreed working position is that if a flow trail has jumps, even rollable ones, it qualifies.
- **Slopestyle features** — included, treated as part of the trails family.
- **Temporary or event spots** — not included unless they have permanent status.
- **Spots that are partly official, partly informal** — list the official elements; do not describe the informal ones.

## Decision principle

When in doubt: would a rider arriving at this location with a jump bike find something useful to ride? If yes, and the location is official, include it.
