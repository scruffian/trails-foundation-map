# Seed data: UK trails spots

List compiled from web research. **All entries need verification before going live** — details may be out of date, and the list is not exhaustive.

## Inclusion rule

A facility qualifies if it has any kind of jumps that a rider with a jump bike, dirt bike or MTB might want to ride. Each spot is tagged with feature types so riders can filter to what they want.

## Feature tags

- **Dirt jumps** — dedicated dirt jump / trails lines
- **Slopestyle** — large built features, often with wooden elements
- **Jump trail** — purpose-built jump line ridden top to bottom
- **Flow trail** — flowy descent with rollable jumps
- **Downhill** — DH trails (may include jumps as obstacles)
- **Freeride** — DH/freeride with big drops, gaps, natural features
- **Pump track**
- **4X / dual slalom**
- **Skills area**

## Other classifications

- **Venue type:** Community / Commercial / Council/Forestry
- **Cost:** Free / Paid / Membership
- **Uplift:** Yes / No

## England — South East

### S4P B1KEPARK, Milford
- **Features:** Dirt jumps
- **Venue:** Commercial (B1KE network)
- **Cost:** Membership
- **Location:** Milford, Surrey
- **Notes:** Dedicated dirt jump park developed by Sam Reynolds, Brendan Fairclough, Olly Wilkins and the S4P crew. Free for B1KE members. Sandy soil rides year-round.

### Wind Hill B1KEPARK
- **Features:** Jump trail, Flow trail, Downhill, Freeride
- **Venue:** Commercial (B1KE network)
- **Cost:** Paid
- **Location:** Hampshire (near Whitchurch)
- **Notes:** Opened April 2017. Known for the Pro-Line designed with Chris Smith. Awarded bike park of the year 2017. Push-up only (no uplift).

### Tidworth B1KEPARK (Tidworth Freeride)
- **Features:** Dirt jumps, Jump trail, Flow trail, Downhill, Freeride, Skills area
- **Venue:** Commercial (B1KE network)
- **Cost:** Paid
- **Location:** Near Tidworth, Wiltshire

## Verification process

Before any spot goes live on the map:

1. Check the spot still exists and is operational
2. Verify feature tags accurately reflect what's there
3. Get current pricing and access information
4. Source at least one decent photo (with permission or licensing)
5. Confirm contact details / social media links work
6. Set "Last verified" date

## Notes on completeness

The bikesandthat.com map likely contains spots not yet captured here. Some indexed park pages confirmed: Pimbo, Rogate, Caersws, Dyfi, Descend. There are almost certainly others on their site that haven't surfaced in search results.

Particular gaps likely include:
- Smaller community-run spots
- Recently opened or rebuilt spots
- Spots in Northern Ireland and remote Scotland
- Council-supported urban facilities with jump features

Community input on these gaps should be a priority after launch.
