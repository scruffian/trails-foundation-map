About

A foundation for grassroots trails.

Trails Foundation is a planned Charitable Incorporated Organisation to support UK dirt jump spots and the communities around them.

What it is

The aim is not to govern the sport, run competitions or create an elite pathway. The aim is practical: help local groups turn fragile informal spots into permanent, publicly accessible places to ride.

The project is currently pre-launch. This website starts with the public map and spot listings because that is immediately useful and helps make the scene visible.

The problem

The pattern is familiar: riders find land, build lines over weeks or months, gather a community, and then lose the spot because the structures around land, insurance, funding and local authority support were never in place.

That loss is not just dirt and timber. It is skilled labour, community, confidence and a safer alternative to riding unmanaged places.

What comes next

The next step is better data: verifying listings, adding real photos, and speaking to riders, clubs and landowners. From there the organisation can develop guidance, case studies and support routes for local groups.
