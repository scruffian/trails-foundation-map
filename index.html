Why trails?

Smooth, flowing dirt.

The word trails has many meanings. In this context we mean smooth, flowing bicycle jumps, berms and rollers, built using dirt.

Trails are:

  • fun to ride
  • fun to build
  • a good excuse to be outside

Why support trails?

Fragile without help.

Trails are often built by volunteers on land they don't own, and this can lead to many problems — vandalism, conflicts with landowners, and many hours of wasted work.

The Trails Foundation aims to support existing trails and trails builders, to create a strong community and enable more people to enjoy trails.
