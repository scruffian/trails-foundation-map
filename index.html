Why it exists

A sport without enough infrastructure.

Riders across the UK build and maintain trails through skilled voluntary work. Too often those spots remain informal, precarious and invisible until they are shut down.

Trails Foundation is being formed to help change that: making existing provision visible, supporting local groups, and giving grassroots trails a clearer route to permanence.
